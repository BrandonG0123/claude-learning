# Brief: Seed to tree

## The idea in one sentence

Growth is slow and then sudden. The spark is the life asleep in the acorn: it wakes with the sprout, waits in the sapling, bursts as the tree's crown, and at year 40 the tree drops its first acorn, so the end is the start.

## Format and spec

- Format: F2 chronology, morph chain — a true process over time, so the worlds are joined by shape morphs (the proven card). Two morphs (the acorn becomes the split shell at 5.0; the seed-leaf becomes the sapling's crown at 9.5) and the one allowed hard cut as the slam on the payoff (the tree at 14.0).
- Size: 1080x1920 (9:16 only), 24fps, 120 BPM, 20.0s = 480 frames = 10 bars. Music only, composed in code. No assets, no brand.
- Style: cut-paper (`piece.json` "style"); hero: the spark (5 rays asleep, 6 at the sprout, 8 in the sapling, 12 as the crown's heart); colour rules: orange = the spark only; green = living growth; brown = the acorn and the wood.
- Clock: a paper tag counts day 1 / week 6 / year 1 → 5 / year 40.

## Sound plan

- Acts for the review: build (seed) → journey (sprout) → peak (years) → silence (hush) → gift (tree) → goodbye (acorn).
- The silence: every tail ends before 13.0 (the pad's release at 12.55, the riser dry-ish and ending at 12.85, no sends in the last bar). The loudest 100 ms window must fall at 14.0. The flat chord 14.3–18.0 sets the gift's p90 above the peak's by ≥ 1 dB; the goodbye drops ≥ 6 dB.

## Claims and sources (web-checked 2026-10-07)

- "week 6" — acorns germinate in 4 to 6 weeks once planted: Morton Arboretum, *Growing Your Own Oak Seedlings* (https://mortonarb.org/app/uploads/2024/10/CRTI_BMP_Appendix-Y_Growing-Your-Own-Oak-Seedlings.pdf). White oaks sown in fall germinate right after sowing: Iowa State University Extension (https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/faq/how-do-i-germinate-acorns).
- "year 40" (the first acorn) — a typical English oak starts producing acorns at around 40 years: Woodland Trust, *Lifecycle of a tree* (https://www.woodlandtrust.org.uk/blog/2019/06/tree-lifecycle/).
- "year 1 → 5" is the story's clock, not a claim; no species is named on screen.
